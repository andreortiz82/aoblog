I've spent twenty years building product experiences — shaping the vision, owning the systems, shipping the work. Lately, almost all of that work runs through an AI agent at some point: Claude Code, mostly, sitting in my editor, reading my files, writing code alongside me. This blog is me trying to articulate that new way of working — in public, as it happens, instead of after the fact.

I'm planning to write about three overlapping threads, plus one side door:

- **Building a design system with AI** — tokens, themes, documentation, tooling.
- **Building a brand with AI** — voice, color, typography, tone, storytelling.
- **Building products with AI** — the day-to-day of shipping software with an agent doing real work.
- **Shorts** — AI-assisted short fiction on the side, sci-fi mostly, some bleak, some hopeful.

Everything you're looking at right now — this post, the site it lives on, the Shorts section — was built in a single session with Claude Code: Astro 5, Tailwind v4, MDX, a couple of content collections, and just enough templates to hold them. It's also deliberately plain right now — system fonts, black/white/gray, no accent, no logo. That's the "before" picture for the brand-design arc that's coming next.

If you want to follow along, the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/andreortiz82/aoblog) is public, including the build plan and the running log of decisions made along the way.

— Andre
