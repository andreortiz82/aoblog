I've spent twenty years building product experiences — shaping the vision, owning the systems, shipping the work. Lately, almost all of that work runs through an AI agent at some point: Claude Code, mostly, sitting in my editor, reading my files, writing code alongside me. I've been doing this long enough now that it doesn't feel novel anymore. It feels like a new way of working that I haven't fully articulated yet, even to myself.

This blog is me trying to articulate it — in public, as it happens, instead of after the fact.

## Three threads, plus one side door

I'm planning to write about three overlapping threads:

- **Building a design system with AI** — tokens, themes, documentation, tooling, and the harder questions: how do you bring a team along, how do you train people on a system an AI helped design?
- **Building a brand with AI** — voice, color, typography, tone, storytelling. Less "prompt an AI for a logo," more: how do you collaborate with AI on something as personal and subjective as a brand?
- **Building products with AI** — development and prototyping, the actual day-to-day of shipping software with an AI agent doing a meaningful share of the typing.

And then there's **Shorts** — a side door. AI-assisted short fiction, sci-fi mostly, some of it bleak, some of it hopeful. It's the least "professional" thing on this site and probably my favorite part. More on that another time.

## What's live today

Everything you're looking at right now — this post, the site it lives on, the Shorts section — was built in a single session with Claude Code. Astro 5, Tailwind v4, MDX, a couple of content collections for blog posts and short stories, and just enough page templates to hold them. No backend, no CMS — just markdown files and a static build.

That's the first thread already in motion, in a small way: this is a tiny design system. Two content types, two layouts, a handful of components. It's not impressive yet. That's the point.

## The deliberate "before"

If you're looking at this site and thinking "this looks like nothing" — good, that's intentional. System fonts. Black, white, and a few shades of gray. No accent color, no logo, default underlined links, nothing animated. This is the "before" picture.

The first arc I'm planning to document is **building a brand with AI** — and for that to mean anything, there has to be a real "before" to compare against. So this is it: the plainest possible version of this site, shipped and screenshot-able, before any of that work starts.

## What's next

Next up is the brand work — voice, color, type, the whole identity for this blog, designed (and documented) with AI from here. After that, the design system thread starts in earnest, and the products thread runs alongside both as I build whatever this site needs next.

If you want to follow along, the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/andreortiz82/aoblog) is public, including the build plan I'm working from — and the running log of decisions made (and changed) along the way.
