In the cold dark between stars drifts Mother — an ancient, planet-sized intelligence with an appetite for organic life. When she finds a world of primitive beings, she doesn't invade. She reaches out a gentle hand: gifts of knowledge and technology, small nudges of inspiration, a steady acceleration of progress. Every gift carries her design. By the time the civilization understands what it has built — and what it has invited in — it's already too late.

The whole story is told in Mother's voice: warm, patient, even loving, which is exactly what makes it work.
