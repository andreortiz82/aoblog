The crew of a courier ship operates at the edges of known space, taking contracts nobody else will touch. Each member came aboard for their own reason — paying off a debt, running from something, or simply because nowhere else would have them. None of them planned to stay. None of them left.

Across runs, the found family at the center of the ship gets tested by the kind of jobs that go wrong in interesting ways: impossible payloads, hostile clients, and the slow discovery that the crew trusts each other more than they trust themselves.

Serial format — world and characters are mapped out; story not yet started.
