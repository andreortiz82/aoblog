Malcolm is a regular guy, lounging in his apartment watching football. He spots a bug, grabs a shoe, and moves in to squash it. Before the blow lands, he's struck by a blinding light — paralyzed, then unconscious. He wakes in an alien court, surrounded by seven-foot insectoid beings draped in robes and jewels: the Blattodea.

The Blattodea are an ancient, subterranean civilization that has covertly shaped life on Earth for 280 million years. Malcolm's crime: killing a hatchling from a royal nest, a capital offense. As the trial unfolds, he learns the truth — humanity has been quietly encouraged to breed and war in an endless cycle, feeding a population that has never stopped growing beneath our feet.

Malcolm pleads. He sobs. He begs for coexistence. The Blattodea are unmoved — there are no exceptions in 280 million years of precedent. In an act that mirrors the one that started everything, he is crushed beneath a mechanical device, his remains fed to a horde of hatchlings.
